Deepward – Privacy Policy

Progressive Zen · Effective 25 October 2026

Deepward is a tower defence game. This policy explains what information the game collects and why. In short: there are no accounts, no ads and no advertising identifiers. To improve the game, it sends anonymous gameplay statistics and crash reports to Google Firebase.

Gameplay statistics (Firebase Analytics)

The game records what happens in play, for example which level you start, whether you win or lose, which towers you build and when you spend gems. It sends these events to Google Firebase Analytics so we can see which levels are too hard or too easy. Firebase adds its own technical data: a random app instance ID, your device model and Android version, the game version, your approximate country (derived from your IP address) and session times. The game does not send your name, email, contacts, precise location or any advertising ID, and the advertising ID permission is removed from the app.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

If the game crashes or hits an error, Firebase Crashlytics sends a report with the error, the place in the game's code where it happened, the level being played, the recent gameplay events and technical details about the device. We use these only to find and fix bugs.

How the data is used and kept

This data is used only to improve and fix Deepward. It is not sold, not shared with other companies for their own purposes and not used for advertising. Google processes it for us as our service provider under the Firebase privacy and security terms. Firebase keeps analytics data for up to 14 months and crash reports for up to 90 days.

Data on your device

Your progress, gems and settings are saved only on your device and are removed when you uninstall the game. There are no user accounts and no cloud saves.

Google Play

Google may collect information when you download or update the game through Google Play, as described in Google's privacy policy. We do not receive personal information from this.

Children

Deepward is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

You can stop all collection by uninstalling the game. To ask about or delete data linked to your app instance, contact us below.
