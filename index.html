Deepward – Privacy Policy

Progressive Zen · Effective 1 November 2026

Deepward is a tower defence game. This policy explains what information the game collects and why. In short: there are no accounts. The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising ID. You can buy "Remove ads" through Google Play. To improve the game, it also sends gameplay statistics and crash reports to Google Firebase.

Gameplay statistics (Firebase Analytics)

The game records what happens in play, for example which level you start, whether you win or lose, which towers you build and when you spend gems. It sends these events to Google Firebase Analytics so we can see which levels are too hard or too easy. Firebase adds its own technical data: a random app instance ID, your device model and Android version, the game version, your approximate country (derived from your IP address) and session times. The game does not send your name, email, contacts or precise location to Firebase.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Deepward shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on some menu screens (never during a level), a short ad after some wins, and optional rewarded ads you choose to watch for a bonus, such as a second chance after a loss or extra starting gold. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects your device's advertising ID, your approximate location (from your IP address), how you interact with ads, and technical details about your device. Google may use this to show personalised ads, depending on your consent. How Google uses this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your consent

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland or a region with similar laws, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings, then Ad privacy. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android device settings (usually Settings, then Google, then Ads, or Settings, then Privacy, then Ads).

In-app purchases (Google Play)

You can buy "Remove ads", which turns off the banner and the ads after wins. Optional rewarded ads stay available. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment card or billing details; we only receive confirmation of which item was bought, so the game can unlock it and restore it after a reinstall.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

If the game crashes or hits an error, Firebase Crashlytics sends a report with the error, the place in the game's code where it happened, the level being played, the recent gameplay events and technical details about the device. We use these only to find and fix bugs.

How the data is used and kept

Gameplay statistics and crash reports are used only to improve and fix Deepward. We do not sell any data. Advertising data is collected and used by Google AdMob as described in the Ads section above. Google processes Firebase data for us as our service provider under the Firebase privacy and security terms. Firebase keeps analytics data for up to 14 months and crash reports for up to 90 days.

Data on your device

Your progress, gems and settings are saved only on your device and are removed when you uninstall the game. There are no user accounts and no cloud saves. A "Remove ads" purchase is kept by Google Play and can be restored from Settings.

Google Play

Google may collect information when you download or update the game through Google Play, as described in Google's privacy policy. We do not receive personal information from this.

Children

Deepward is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

You can change your ad consent in Settings, then Ad privacy, and manage your advertising ID in your device settings. You can stop all collection by uninstalling the game. To ask about or delete data linked to your app instance, contact us below.
